# IDQA v0.1 — Initial Public Research Record

This release establishes the first public working specification of **Intent-Driven Quality Assurance (IDQA)**.

## Included

- public methodology overview;
- Working Specification and Research Agenda v0.1;
- citation metadata with ORCID;
- CC BY-NC 4.0 license for the public methodology text;
- changelog and public research status.

## Core working chain

    Intent
      → Guarantees
      → Evidence Requirements
      → Evidence Strategies
      → Observations
      → Evidence
      → Assurance

## Status

IDQA is a **working research proposal under empirical evaluation**. This release does not claim peer review, proven superiority, or established cross-domain generality.
